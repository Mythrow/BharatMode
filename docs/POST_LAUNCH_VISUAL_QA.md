Main story art is constructed at W=540,H=960; Pillow raster labels are drawn at that resolution, then `cv2.resize(...,INTER_LINEAR)` enlarges to 1080×1920. Small text and lines lose detail. YouTube transcode may add degradation; no platform stream independently inspected. Not all title/text layers originated at half resolution.
