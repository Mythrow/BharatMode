# Bharat Mode — operating blueprint (November 2026)

## Brand idea
Bharat Mode is a **state of making things better**: building, creating, discovering, inventing. Signature phrase: **MODE: ON**. Tagline: **Big Ideas. Real Progress.**

## Promise to viewers
'Discover the breakthroughs, builders and progress shaping India — with evidence and context.' Inform rather than advertise. Shorts only initially.

## Pillars and starting mix
- 30% science and inventions
- 20% engineering, manufacturing and infrastructure
- 15% public-service and quality-of-life improvements
- 15% inspiring people / unsung innovators
- 10% economy, exports and measurable outcomes
- 10% space, environment and global collaboration

## Initial cadence
Two Shorts per weekday, one per weekend day if the story pipeline supports quality. Maintain a seven-day verified story buffer and avoid filler. Test 35–55 seconds normally; longer only when the explanation requires it.

## Repeatable formats
- 'You Missed This' — overlooked discovery
- 'Built in Bharat' — engineering in practice
- 'One Number That Matters' — grounded data
- 'Who Made This?' — people-driven achievement
- 'What's Next?' — promise versus current development stage

## Production pipeline
Discover → dedupe → verify → storyboard → original script → editorial review → source and asset licensing check → natural voice and captions → Remotion graphics → render → device QA → human approval → YouTube upload → performance review.

## Story score out of 25
Score each 0–5: surprising; sourced; genuinely meaningful; easy to explain; visually depictable. Aim for 18+ and stop publication on factual or rights red flags.

## Narrative arc
0–3s unexpected hook → 3–12s precise event → 12–30s evidence → 30–43s why it matters → final takeaway and subtle 'Mode: ON' signature.

## Measurement
Viewer stays/swipes, average view percentage, shares, saves where available, subscribers per 1,000 views and returning audience. Compare by topic, length and hook — do not chase views at the expense of accuracy.

## Operational limits
This repo is a starter for semi-automated production, **not** an automated news publisher. A human approves the published facts, visuals and rights. YouTube may consider repetitive mass-produced slides/news reading low-value for monetization.
