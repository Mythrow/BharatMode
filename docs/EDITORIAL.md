# Editorial standards — Bharat Mode

**Mission:** Find compelling, under-covered Indian progress stories. Be optimistic without becoming uncritical.

## Canonical story identity and duplicate control

Before registering a new Short, **search every stage** of the permanent [Bharat Mode Content Log](../content/CONTENT_LOG.md) and its [registry](../content/registry.json), including Published. Every credible, distinct candidate gets exactly one `BM-####` ID, research sheet and unique event/story key; completed videos keep the same ID forever. Use [Content Pipeline](CONTENT_PIPELINE.md) for intake, cross-linked follow-up events and status gates. Same news from a different article is **not** a new Short. A draft script must declare its `story.contentId`. Update existing content IDs instead of creating near-duplicates.

## Required per-story record
1. Canonical primary URL, issuing institution and publication date.
2. Actual date the event took place, plus the **as-of date** for every figure.
3. Clear claim stage: proposed / approved / funded / researched / tested / licensed / deployed / independently measured.
4. Original source excerpt or link and a second corroborating source for contested or unusually large claims.
5. Explicit caveat about what is **not** yet proven.
6. Rights and licensing provenance for any third-party imagery, sounds or footage.
7. Script, draft visuals, pronunciation notes and a human sign-off.

## Sources
Prefer papers, university labs, ISRO, CSIR, ICMR, DST, ICAR, MoSPI, RBI, independent datasets, government dashboards, credible local reporting and original innovators. PIB releases are useful leads but are government claims; verify adoption and impact separately. Social media is a **lead**, not proof.

## Language rules
- Research breakthrough ≠ commercial product.
- Patent ≠ market adoption.
- MoU ≠ completed investment.
- Technology transferred ≠ approved medical device.
- Announced project ≠ opened infrastructure.
- Expected benefit ≠ measured impact.
- Avoid 'world first', 'never before', 'India number one' unless robustly sourced.
- No party-political campaigning; no unsupported national comparisons.
- No photorealistic generated event footage passed off as documentary evidence.
- Disclose meaningful AI-generated or altered media, as YouTube requires.
- Attribute primary evidence in the description; attribution is **not** permission to reuse footage.

## Research notes: examples
### PARAM Rudra
Source: https://www.pib.gov.in/PressNoteDetails.aspx?ModuleId=3&NoteId=160306&lang=1&reg=48

The source reports **6,000 Indian-designed Rudra servers deployed by September 2026** and **1,500 under manufacturing**. Its **more than 1.5 crore computing jobs** pertains to the wider National Supercomputing Mission — not exclusively Rudra servers.

### ICMR
Source: https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=2337773&lang=1&reg=1

ICMR reported 100 cumulative technology-transfer deals and 20 licensing agreements associated with eight technologies and nine companies at the November 2026 summit. **Licensing is not proof of a commercially available or approved product.**

Before publication, independently reconfirm every quoted claim against the live source and label government-reported statistics accordingly.
