# ID-006 — India approves ₹11,000 crore construction equipment manufacturing scheme

Stage: In Progress / BM-005. Original event and primary source date: 25 November 2026. Discovered and selected 26 November 2026.

## Initial evidence

Cabinet approved ₹11,000 crore incentive scheme on 25 November. India’s CIE market was about ₹97,500 crore in FY2024-25, third largest globally according to government; Reuters reports 45% of demand supplied by imports. Covers advanced machinery including tunnel boring machines, port cranes and excavators. Two-year setup period FY27–28, five-year incentive period FY29–33, settlement FY34. It is an approved scheme, not machinery delivered or import substitution already achieved. Strong engineering visual angle: what giant machines build India's infrastructure and why manufacturing them domestically matters. Primary PIB indexed text available but opening direct page failed; corroborated by Akashvani and Reuters.

## Stage 01 detailed research — 26 November 2026
Owner selected ID-006 for BM-005. Cabinet approval on 25 November 2026, official PIB Cabinet release 15:23 IST and MHI release 15:35 IST. Ministry of Heavy Industries implements the Scheme for Enhancement of Construction and Infrastructure Equipment (CIE) Manufacturing Ecosystem. Confirmed approved outlay ₹11,000 crore (not disbursed expenditure). FY2026–27 to FY2033–34: two setup years FY27–28, five incentive years FY29–33, final settlement FY34. Sales-linked incentives on incremental eligible sales for approved OEMs and component manufacturers, not all revenue; Cabinet briefing PDF pp4–6 specifies equipment 13–10%, components 11–8%. Testing facility grant up to ₹375 crore (within total scheme, not additional) to ARAI as viability gap funding. Facility planned, not operational. Seven selected high-import-dependence equipment categories: TBMs, off-highway trucks, excavators, cranes, loaders, piling rigs, drilling machines. Five component categories: engines, transmissions, hydraulic systems, undercarriages, drive systems. Do not imply all machines in each category imported or that India never produced these machines. Government FY25 market estimate ₹97,500 crore, world's third largest after China and USA; about 45% import dependence per Cabinet briefing (estimate and scope as reported, not a unit share or China-only share). FY30 market projected ₹2.28 lakh crore as reported by Moneycontrol/TOI; not actual. Cabinet expects ₹21,000 crore investment, ₹22,500 crore import substitution over scheme period and almost 4x exports over five years; all expectations not achieved. Detailed eligibility, base year, annual rates, applicants, awarded beneficiaries and actual disbursements not verified. Reuters and Business Standard report Chinese export licensing controls on TBMs effective 18 Nov 2026; licensing not blanket ban, scheme was in development before restrictions. Keep geopolitical context secondary. Distinguish existing manufacturing capabilities from proposed deepening. Avoid numerical claims about jobs not verified. 

Recommended achievement-first hook: India has approved an eleven-thousand-crore push to manufacture more of the giant machines that build its infrastructure. Story angle: the machines behind the infrastructure; TBM cutterhead and tunnel, excavator, ship-to-shore crane; explain component supply chain and sales-linked incentives/testing, ending with potential stronger domestic supply. Prefer actual grounded machinery geometry and cleared imagery, animated independent components, distinguish projected results visually. No official machine/company brands implied as beneficiaries.
