# BM-007 visual storyboard V3 — camera and map correction

Run `python make_gallery.py` in this source directory (Python, Pillow, CairoSVG and Inter fallback). `python test_camera.py` checks all 3,300 provisional frame states, camera keyframes, graphic clipping and text separation, conservative transformed fleet bounds, and source-based city pin coordinates. `python render_motion.py START END output.mp4` creates a native 60 FPS silent review segment (frame range start inclusive/end exclusive). Use ranges 480:1200, 1200:1620 and 2820:3300 for scenes 2, 3 and 6. These segments are deliberately hard-cut review clips, not the finished video. Actual narration, acoustic captions, narration-aligned camera cues, final dissolve and music remain pending.

V2 was rejected: manually drawn India outline, dimension line crossing numeric labels, unwanted blue polygon, headline retained during camera close-up, and closing line intersecting formation. V3 uses a clipped world-coordinate camera layer with separate screen-space text; the editorial heading leaves before the close-up and restores after the wide shot, while fixed brand chrome and captions remain. The ruler scans bow-to-stern, the numeric measurement never resets, and the final two values occupy their own band. The map goes wide -> Mumbai -> pan to Kolkata -> wide. Each ship enters only after its transformed full bounds fit in the fleet region. The central ship is one continuous object, not a crossfade between duplicate graphics. Closing text is below the camera clipping boundary.

## Map provenance

`map.json` contains a source-projected outline based on the geoBoundaries ADM0 extract distributed in https://github.com/anjishnuHyperworks/india-basemap/blob/main/data/india-country-boundary.geojson (blob 5c212752d587bb2acb5cd7009d9bfef09f731571). Repository README attributes the country polygon to geoBoundaries (Runfola et al.), CC BY 4.0. The repository's LICENSE has a general ODbL data notice; verify the upstream attribution/license before publication. The country geometry represents India's claimed extent, including the northern regions; not an authenticated Survey of India or survey-grade boundary. Projected x=540+(lon−82)*16.5, y=920+(22−lat)*18, Douglas–Peucker simplified at 1.15 design pixels and rounded for native SVG; subpixel island rings are omitted. City pins use the same projection of Mumbai 72.8777E/19.076N and Kolkata 88.3639E/22.5726N. Attribution and changes must be included in the publication description: geoBoundaries (Runfola et al.), CC BY 4.0, via india-basemap; projected and simplified. Do not use hand-drawn outlines or describe this as a certified official map. Official Survey of India boundary data could not be retrieved into the rendering environment during this revision.

Original editable conceptual ship illustrations, not engineering drawings or authentic photographs. Same approved avatar, Inter fallback, approved 125-word script. No audio generated; estimated 55-second storyboard. Owner review and final production QA pending.
