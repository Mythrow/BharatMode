## Editorial angle
How Indian medical research moves toward real-world products. Explain what technology licensing means without treating it as a regulatory approval or as proof of patient impact.

## Verified lead
Primary source: [PIB — ICMR health research summit, 18 November 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=2337773&lang=1&reg=1).

- ICMR announced **100 cumulative technology transfer deals**. This is not 100 new deals announced on 18 November.
- At the summit, **20 licensing agreements** covered **eight technologies** with **nine Indian companies**.
- **17 indigenous health technologies were showcased**; showcasing is not a confirmation of clinical or market adoption.

## Caveats / unresolved questions
- No basis to say every technology is medically approved or commercially available.
- Source is a government announcement. Seek evidence from licensees, trial records or regulators for any eventual impact claim.
- Existing storyboard and reported milestone require final live fact review before production.
