### Verified claim ledger
- Ministry of Defence / PIB 2342700 (27 Nov 2026, 18:04 IST): Vindhyagiri accepted/delivered at GRSE Kolkata on 27 Nov; seventh and final Nilgiri-class frigate. Delivery is distinct from commissioning. No claim of final vessel operational or all ships delivered on the same day.
- Same release: first delivery 20 Dec 2024, all seven deliveries completed in about 23 months. This is the delivery span, not total construction duration. Build periods: first ship 84 months, final ship 68 months (16 months shorter). Project-level indigenous content 75%; over 200 MSMEs; approximately 4,000 direct and over 10,000 indirect employment opportunities. Do not equate indigenous content with every component Indian or combine employment into a verified exact total.
- GRSE / PIB 2342521 (27 Nov, 14:41 IST, accessible lang=2): 149 m length and 6,670 tonnes for GRSE P17A ships; third of three GRSE frigates, 121st warship delivered by GRSE. Navy Warship Design Bureau designed class. Broad stealth/survivability and multi-role capabilities; no tactical vulnerabilities or speculative performance claims.
- PIB 2257237 (1 May 2026): sixth ship Mahendragiri was fourth MDL-built ship, confirming 4 Mumbai / 3 Kolkata division with final GRSE announcement.
- Times of India 27 Nov reports approx. Rs 45,000 crore total programme value. Not necessary for core script; retain attribution if used, no assertion of audited final expenditure.

### Proposed story treatment
1. Opening: final ship delivered; seven out of seven milestone, authentic or clearly conceptual frigate visual from opening frame.
2. What it is: Indian-designed advanced stealth frigate, 149 m, 6,670 tonnes; avoid calling it invisible.
3. Two yards: four ships from Mazagon Dock, Mumbai, three from GRSE, Kolkata; no incorrect ship silhouettes or individual identities.
4. Delivery span and learning: 23 months between first and last deliveries; 84 vs 68 month build time comparison.
5. Industrial ecosystem: 75% indigenous content, 200+ MSMEs; project employment if timing permits.
6. Close: domestic warship design/build capability and completed deliveries; commissioning distinction.
