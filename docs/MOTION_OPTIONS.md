# Bharat Mode — Motion Alternatives, HTML Review

**Status:** review candidates, not approved final motion rules. Three interactive HTML documents are self-contained (the exact approved BM avatar is embedded as SVG data); voice and music are not included, and captions are illustrative rather than synced to narration. Use a browser to open the HTML directly without installing code.

## Open the examples

- [A — Quiet Editorial](../previews/html/bharat-mode-a-quiet-editorial.html): ivory, soft fades and rising text, solid charcoal caption band, minimal chapter progress.
- [B — Cinematic Data](../previews/html/bharat-mode-b-cinematic-data.html): deep charcoal, stronger numerical reveal, schematic graphics, translucent light captions.
- [C — Saffron Process](../previews/html/bharat-mode-c-saffron-process.html): saffron highlight, directional motion, illustrated process stages, floating light caption pill.
- [Comparison page](../previews/html/bharat-mode-motion-comparison.html): embedded previews for all three; download the **entire** `previews/html` folder to preserve iframe links.

Each document supports Play/Pause, Replay, speed adjustment and timeline scrubbing. User does not need to run Remotion locally.

## Principles applied to all three
1. Original approved BM avatar from `public/brand/bharat-mode-avatar.svg`, with no redraw/recolor.
2. Contrasting single-topic `SCIENCE` pill, no serial number.
3. Three example editorial beats are **not a mandated chapter count**. Future Shorts group scenes based on their actual story and evidence.
4. Segmented chapter bar near lower safe region: no persistent source/date/India-innovation footer.
5. Abstract decorations stay subtle. Real source/event photography must be rights-cleared before publication.
6. The example claim is government-reported deployment of 6,000 Rudra servers as of September 2026, recorded in `stories/param-rudra.json`. Date appears only with the qualifying claim.
7. Sora and Manrope are loaded from Google Fonts when the browser is online. Offline browser fallback may alter text metrics.
8. No sound/voice is embedded, and these previews are **not publishable Shorts**.

## Review checklist
- Which headline entrance is preferred: soft-rise, numerical/masked, or directional?
- Prefer solid captions, translucent light captions or floating pill?
- How strong should accent-background movement be?
- Should different kinds of stories use a different treatment? (Recommended: yes.)
- Are caption safe-zones and chapter bar readable at small screen size?
- After approval: carry selected patterns into actual Remotion sequences, verify Sora/Manrope render, add voice-synced captions, original audio masters and mobile QA.

The source docs are not renders and the HTML files are only review prototypes. Actual finished MP4s and voice remain excluded from Git.
