Owner selected ID-009 as BM-006. Event 25 Nov at Bharat Mandapam (developer event listing 1–4:30 PM; contemporaneous ET EnterpriseAI 18:24 IST and ANI evening coverage); official PIB 26 Nov 11:23 IST. Do not confuse official publication with launch date. Chariot Artificial Intelligence (formerly Gan.ai, cofounders Parth Sarthi and Suvrat Bhooshan) among first four IndiaAI foundation model teams. The LLM and TTS suite reportedly pretrained from scratch in India; not evidence of all-Indian hardware/data or India's first LLM. Largest LLM 10.7B (rounded 11B) MoE, 4B active per token; smaller variants 1.7B/745M. Active parameters do not determine resident memory or intelligence; actual routing, context, training data, checkpoints, inference requirements unverified. Three research techniques: IndicShift shared reversible script representation, 1,012 characters across 12 scripts represented by 350 letters; official reported up to 2.5x reduction in token count vs GPT tokenizer (up to 60% fewer tokens; comparator version/corpus not specified in release), 5–8% Indian-language performance gain on same compute, English/code maintained in reported tests. Nested Training shares one training run/weight set across three sizes; reported 62% higher training speed vs separate training. Mixture of Vocabularies groups vocabulary into 11 groups, evaluates relevant group; reported 5.7x reduction in scored entries and 38% training-speed gain on same GPUs with negligible change in loss. These are distinct developer-reported experiments; no additive gains, universal speed, inference or price claims. PIB describes IndicShift as open source; HF indexed listing now shows chariot-ai/indic-shift-262k, secondary report describes 131k/262k repositories and small token test; direct repository inaccessible, license/code not independently verified. No publicly downloadable Chariot-1 LLM weights, model card, license or public LLM API verified in this search. Do not call entire suite open source, locally runnable or better than ChatGPT. TTS service/API advertised by developer (not independently tested). PIB lists English + Hindi, Bengali, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Gujarati, Punjabi, Odia. Developer multilingual TTS launch 12 Nov lists 11 Indic languages plus English (includes Urdu); scope discrepancy, use at least ten Indian languages plus English, as reported at showcase. ASR Realtime announced 22 Oct, not new at showcase. TTS context-aware numbers/dates/currency/units/abbreviations, code-mixing advertised. PIB reports >97% verbalisation accuracy on Async benchmark (1029 sentences, 2251 nonstandard instances, 42 categories), but developer 12 Nov multilingual evaluation reports 93.78% English verbalisation accuracy; version/scope difference unresolved, omit headline benchmark. Hindi CER 7.60%, nine-language avg 6.47% reported tests, not overall quality ranking. Speech-native reasoning is broader research direction, not verified universally deployed ability.
