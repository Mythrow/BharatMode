## What happened / why this matters

Official Ministry of Science and Technology release confirms 27 Nov inauguration of high-containment research facility in Lucknow. Intended to strengthen infectious disease research including tuberculosis. Institute's historical 13 marketed drugs and 80+ licensed process technologies are not outputs of the new facility. New infrastructure, not new treatment or cure. Proposed title: CSIR Opens New Drug Research Lab.
